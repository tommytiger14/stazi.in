Common Questions

How much does it cost?

We offer two active plans. Core is 5% of your host payout — we set up your listing and pricing once. Plus is 10% — we actively manage pricing and listing performance every week. A Full-Service plan at 25% is coming soon. All plans are commission-only; you pay us only when Airbnb pays you. No setup fees.

Do I need to provide linens?

We can use your existing supply, or we can coordinate professional linen rental services for a hotel-quality experience.

Can I still use my property?

Absolutely! It's your home. Just block off the dates you want on the calendar, and we won't book guests for those days.
